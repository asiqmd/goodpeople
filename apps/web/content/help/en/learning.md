**Learning** comes with the Business plan and up. Employees find their required training, available sessions and completion records there, on the web and in the app. HR officers, HR admins and workspace admins manage learning for their companies. Managers see required-training coverage for their direct reports.

## Courses and sessions

HR creates a course with English and Bangla names and descriptions, a category and an optional renewal period in months. A blank period means no expiry. These are company policies, not automatic statutory requirements. Archive a course instead of deleting its history; only an HR login with access to all companies can archive the shared catalogue.

Schedule a session for one company and optionally a site, with its trainer, venue, start/end times and capacity. Web forms use Dhaka time. HR enrols employees by employee code. A person must work for that company on the session date. Employees can enrol themselves and withdraw before the start. Capacity is enforced even when people enrol at the same time. A cancelled session is retained and its enrolments are withdrawn.

## Attendance and completion records

After a session ends, HR records every enrolled person as **attended** or **absent**, with a note about the signed attendance register. Completing the session fixes it. Attendees receive numbered completion records; absent people do not. HR can enter a historical register, but cannot complete future training.

Completion records keep the course name, trainer, completion date and expiry policy at the time they are issued. Download an English or Bangla PDF from the record. It records attendance entered by the employer and does not claim external accreditation. PDFs are generated from the record and are not separate uploads in the document vault.

An incorrect record is **revoked with a reason**, never deleted. Revoked records remain readable and their PDFs are marked revoked, but they never count as current training. Corrections require a new session/attendance record. Course editing and assessment delivery are outside this milestone; archive and create a new course when the policy changes.

## Required training

HR requires a course for a designation in a company, from an effective date, with an optional end and grace period. Overlapping requirements for the same company, designation and course are refused.

Grace starts from the later of the policy date and the start of the employee's continuous time in that company/designation. A new designation or company restarts that clock; unrelated changes in the same designation do not. A completion counts on or before the selected reporting date, through its inclusive expiry date, unless revoked. The view shows **current**, **due**, or **overdue**. Current people, including workers without logins, appear in HR's coverage view.

The daily job reminds enrolled employees about sessions within the next 24 hours and certificates expiring within 30 days, then once after expiry. Notifications open Learning in the app. It does not silently enrol people or certify them.

## Fire drills and buyer audits

Record a drill after it happens: company/site, participants, evacuation time, conductor, findings, corrective actions and next due date. Void mistakes with a reason and enter a corrected record. Keep signed registers and notices required for the establishment.

The official Labour Act's s.62(8) requires a fire drill at least every six months for establishments with 50 or more workers/employees. Set the next date according to all applicable requirements, fire-licence conditions and buyer standards; counsel or the safety lead confirms those requirements. Good People records evidence and does not declare legal compliance.

The buyer-audit report includes required-training coverage at the period end, valid completion counts in the period, missing/overdue training and recorded drills up to the period end. Company-limited logins see their companies only. The training section is available with Learning and appears in the English/Bangla PDF as well as the web report.
