A free trial gives your company 30 days of everything in the Business plan: core HR, leave, attendance, payroll with salary tax, piece rates, projects and timesheets, worker kiosks and groups of companies. No card is needed, and you can export your data at any time.

## Start the trial

1. On the [sign-up page](/signup), enter your name, your work email, the company name and its industry, and accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
2. We email you a six-digit code. It works for 15 minutes and allows five wrong guesses.
3. Enter the code, choose the **Workspace address** your team will sign in with (we suggest a free one from the company name) and a password of at least 10 characters.

The workspace is created and you are signed in as its **Workspace admin**. Where the server requires two-step sign-in for admins, you set it up first: see [signing in and two-step sign-in](/help/sign-in-and-two-step). Each email address gets one trial; if yours already has one, we email you a reminder to sign in instead.

## What your workspace starts with

Every workspace gets the Bangladesh country pack: casual, sick, annual and maternity leave; this year's and next year's public holidays, with lunar dates marked tentative; six standard letters in English and Bangla; pay components and salary structures; and a general shift.

The industry you chose then adds a starting organisation:

- **RMG / manufacturing:** the company, a head office and a factory; Admin & HR, Finance, Production (Cutting, Sewing with Lines 1 and 2, Finishing) and Quality; operator, line and quality designations; a day shift and a night shift.
- **Corporate / IT:** the company and a head office with Friday and Saturday off; HR & Admin, Finance, Sales, Operations and Technology; executives, managers and officers; office hours.
- **NGO:** a head office and a field office; Programmes, Finance & Admin, Monitoring & Evaluation and Human Resources; programme officers, field facilitators and coordinators.
- **Group of companies:** the group's first company and a corporate office; Group HR, Group Finance and Shared Services.
- **Other:** the company and a head office; HR & Admin and Finance.

These are ordinary records: rename, add or remove them on **Organization**.

## First steps

After sign-up you land on the [welcome page](/workspace/welcome), which the trial banner also links to as **Getting started**. It lists five steps, each marked **Done** from your data:

1. **Check the company details**: the legal name, TIN and registration number go on letters, payslips and registers.
2. **Review your organisation**: the sites, departments, lines and designations from the template.
3. **Add your employees**: [import them from Excel](/help/import-employees), or add a few by hand.
4. **Give your HR team logins** on the **Users** page.
5. **Set up pay**: check the salary structures and give everyone their salary.

Then open the [Go-live checklist](/workspace/setup). It shows what is left before people rely on Good People: geofences, employee logins, two-step sign-in for admins, shifts, devices and device numbers, holidays, leave types, email and SMS delivery, and, with payroll, approved pay, payment accounts and parallel runs. Each step is read from your data, so it is always up to date, and **Fix** opens the page where you fix it.

## When the trial ends

Every page shows **Free trial: N days left**, and the people who manage billing get reminders a week before and the day before it ends. To carry on, a workspace admin or a finance officer chooses a plan on **Billing**: see [plans and billing](/help/plans-and-billing).

If the trial ends without a plan, the workspace becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted or locked away: everyone can still sign in, see everything and export it, and Billing stays open, so choosing a plan gives full access back at once.
