HR sets up leave on **Leave settings**. Employees apply on **Leave**, in the mobile app or at a kiosk, and managers and HR decide in **Approvals**.

## The leave types you start with

Every workspace gets the Labour Act's leave types, at the law's values:

- **Casual leave:** 10 days a year, not carried forward (s.115).
- **Sick leave:** 14 days a year, not carried forward, with a certificate after 2 days (s.116).
- **Annual (earned) leave:** earned by days worked, one day for every 18 days in offices and shops, and one for every 14 in factories since 17 November 2025. It can be taken after a year's continuous service, and up to 40 unused days carry forward (s.117).
- **Maternity leave:** per birth, for women with six months' service, counted in calendar days: 120 days since 17 November 2025, 112 before (s.46, s.47).

**Labour Act rules in force**, at the foot of the page, shows each rule's section and whether it still needs legal review. **Add missing Labour Act types** puts back any that were removed.

The law is a floor: a statutory type can be more generous, but a setting below the law is refused, quoting the section. **Add a company leave type** for anything else, paid or unpaid.

## How each type works

Open a type to change it. **Accrual** is one of:

- **Yearly allowance:** the **Days a year** for each calendar year, pro-rated in the year someone joins.
- **Earned by days worked:** last year's days worked divided by **Days worked per leave day**. Left empty, it follows the Labour Act for each location's kind of site. Where attendance is tracked, the days actually worked count; before that, scheduled working days.
- **Per event:** each request may have up to **Most days per request**, as for maternity.

Each type also has a **Carry-forward cap**, **Minimum service (days)**, **Only for** (one gender), **Count weekly offs and holidays**, **Allow half days** and **Certificate after (days)**. In a group of companies, a company can differ from the group on days a year, carry-forward and days per request, on **Group → Policies**, never below the law.

## Approvals

**Approval steps** are the type's chain of **Manager** and **HR**, in order. A manager without a login is skipped so a request never gets stuck; if that leaves nobody, an HR step is added. A type with no steps is approved at once. Nobody decides their own request, and a rejection ends the chain.

## Holidays and weekly offs

Under **Holiday calendars**, keep one calendar or several, with one as the default. **Import Bangladesh holidays** adds the year's national days and estimated Eid, Puja and other lunar dates, marked **Tentative** until the moon is sighted; then **Confirm date**. Each holiday is public, festival, optional or company, and the page warns when festival holidays fall short of the 13 days the Act requires. Under **Locations**, each location picks its calendar and weekly off days, Friday unless you change it.

Changing a holiday recounts the leave requests it overlaps, and approved leave gets a correction in the balance.

## Balances and requests

Balances are worked out, not stored: what someone is entitled to, plus what was carried forward and HR's adjustments, less what was taken and what is waiting. HR enters opening balances and other changes with **Adjust balance** on the employee's **Leave** tab, always with a note.

A request counts working days only, unless the type counts offs and holidays. It is refused when it overlaps another request, crosses 31 December (split it into two), needs more days than are available, comes before the minimum service, or breaks one of the type's settings. A waiting request can be cancelled at any time; approved leave only before it starts, and its days go back to the balance. Maternity cases and their benefit are on the **Maternity** page.
