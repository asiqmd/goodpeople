**Engagement** and **Employee services** are included with Core HR, on web and mobile. HR officers, HR admins and workspace admins manage their own authorized companies on the web. Your line manager does not gain access to your private HR requests.

## Anonymous surveys and eNPS

HR drafts a bilingual survey for one company. The eNPS question asks how likely you are to recommend it as a workplace, from 0 to 10. Optional pulse questions use 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Answer every question and submit once before the closing time. You cannot change an anonymous response or answer for another employee.

Publishing freezes the questions, active employee audience, deadline and minimum reporting threshold. The threshold is at least five people; HR cannot publish a smaller audience. Individual answers and their timestamps are never stored. A participation marker prevents duplicate submissions; totals are updated separately in the same transaction. The app does not expose participant lists or raw-response exports.

Only authorized HR can see results, after closing and enough responses. Until then, even the response count is withheld. Results cover the fixed company cohort, without department, demographic or date filters. eNPS is the percentage scoring 9–10 minus the percentage scoring 0–6; 7–8 are passive. Pulse results show averages and score counts. Closing reminders go to everyone in the audience so the delivery log does not identify respondents. A reminder does not mean you have not answered.

This protects the product's reporting; it does not promise anonymity from a database administrator observing live transactions. Workers without a login cannot respond by proxy. Arrange a suitable separate channel for them instead of inventing responses.

## Recognition

Use a colleague's employee code, choose teamwork, service, quality or initiative, and write your message. Your name and message are visible to your company. You can withdraw your own recognition with a reason. HR can moderate recognition within its company scope. Withdrawn messages stay on record but disappear from the feed. You cannot recognize yourself or someone outside your current company. The feed shows the latest 100 posts; there are no rewards or salary effects.

## Announcements and policy notices

HR creates English and Bangla wording for one company. General announcements are visible to current employees, including new joiners. A policy notice freezes an acknowledgement audience at publication; HR must issue a new notice for later joiners. Open the notice and read all the text before acknowledging it. Your receipt says you read it; it is not agreement to new employment terms or an electronic signature.

HR can see the assigned, acknowledged and outstanding register. A late acknowledgement is allowed while the notice is live. Published wording cannot be changed: retire it with a reason and publish a replacement. Retired or expired notices retain their history and cannot accept new receipts. This milestone publishes plain text, without attachments.

## Private requests to your HR team

Open a request for letters, pay, leave, benefits or another ordinary HR service. Choose normal or urgent priority, write the subject and details, and send. Only you and authorized HR handlers of the request's company can read it. This is separate from contacting Good People platform support. For a statutory grievance or harassment concern, use your workspace's dedicated grievance channel; ordinary requests have no automatically inferred statutory deadline.

Your subject and messages are encrypted. Notifications contain a generic link, never request details. HR can assign an active authorized handler, reply publicly, or keep an internal note that you cannot see. Internal notes do not send you a notification or change your visible activity time. HR marks the request in progress or resolved with an explanation. You may close a resolved request, or reopen a resolved/closed one with a reason. Closed requests cannot receive replies until reopened. If you transfer companies, the original request stays with its original HR team.

## Issued assets

See your own current and returned issues in Employee services. Open an issue to read the tag, dates, condition and due date, then acknowledge an outstanding receipt. HR records the actual issue and return dates and conditions. Only one person can hold an asset at once. Historical overlaps and future event dates are refused; an outstanding asset must be returned before it can be retired. Your history remains after return or transfer.

Receipt is a record, not a salary deduction. Procurement, depreciation, financial recovery and offline proxy receipts are outside this milestone. HR authoring and inventory remain on the web; the mobile app provides employee self-service. Read-only workspaces keep records readable and refuse changes.
