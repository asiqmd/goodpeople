A group of companies runs in one workspace: every sister concern as a company under **Organization**, one set of policies, and everyone in one place. More than one company comes with the Business plan, for up to 10 companies; Enterprise has no limit.

## Moving people between companies

A move to another company is a **Transfer**, recorded with **Record a change** on the person's page. They keep the same record and joining date, so their service stays continuous.

## The group dashboard

**Group** → **Dashboard** shows a month for each company beside the group's total:

- headcount, joiners, leavers and attrition over twelve months;
- women's share and average service;
- overtime and absence from processed attendance;
- open compliance alerts;
- payroll cost, only for those who may see pay.

Below it, a twelve-month headcount trend comes with the same figures as a table.

## Group policy and company overrides

What the workspace sets is the group's policy. Under **Group** → **Policies**, a company may set its own values in four areas:

- **Leave**: each leave type's days a year, carry-forward and most days per request;
- **Punching from the app**: whether people punch from the web and mobile apps, and whether a photo is needed;
- **Provident fund rates**: contributions in percent of basic;
- **Overtime caps**: the company's own caps for the compliance check.

Leave a field empty to follow the group. Anything not overridden follows the group, so a change to group policy reaches every company that has not chosen otherwise. Statutory leave cannot go below the Labour Act's minimum, and overtime caps cannot go above the law's limits. Leave balances, punching, payroll and the working-time checks each read the values of the employee's own company.

> A leave override applies from the next accrual or request. Balances already granted are not recomputed.

## Shared-service HR

On **Users**, a login can be limited to some of the group's companies: choose its companies and save. With none ticked, it sees every company. The workspace admin always sees every company.

A limited login sees only its companies' people, team views, HR's pages for one person, leave and correction lists, rosters, documents, letters and ID cards, probation and separations, compliance, the group dashboard and the approvals HR decides. People in other companies are simply not found.

> Payroll is not limited by company, because a pay group can span companies: a payroll login sees every company's pay.
