Good People works out each person's salary income tax in every payroll run, using the Income Tax Act 2023 and the tax years in the Bangladesh country pack (July to June). It is on by default, under **Payroll** → **Settings** → **Salary tax and provident fund**.

## How a month's tax is worked out

For each person, the run projects the salary for the whole income year:

- what locked runs already paid this year, including adjustments and festival bonuses, plus any figures from before Good People;
- this month;
- their regular pay for the months left until June, or until a separation already recorded.

From that it takes the year's tax: the exempt part of salary (a third, at most Tk 5 lakh), the tax-free threshold for the person, the bands, the investment rebate and the minimum tax. It subtracts the tax already deducted and spreads the rest over the months left, this one included (s.86). A raise, a bonus or a new declaration evens out over the following months.

The working is kept with the run: open a person and click **How the tax of … was worked out**; employees see the same under **My pay** → **Tax**. Payroll can override one person's tax for a month by entering an amount for it on the run.

## What payroll enters

On an employee's **Pay** tab:

- **Tax profile**: a disability, a gazetted war-wounded freedom fighter or July fighter, and dependants with a disability (each adds Tk 50,000 to the threshold; only one parent claims). Gender and age come from the employee record.
- **Before this system, this year**: salary, tax and provident fund paid this income year before the first run here, so the year's tax comes out right.

A TIN missing where tax is deducted shows as a warning on the run.

## Investment declarations

Employees declare their investments for the year under **My pay** → **Tax** → **Investments for the rebate**, and click **Send to payroll** (or save a draft). Payroll can also enter them on the employee's **Pay** tab.

Declared amounts count straight away. Once payroll has seen the proofs, it opens **Payroll** → **Tax**, enters the accepted amount for each item and clicks **Verify**. From then on only the accepted amounts count, and the declaration is closed. DPS counts up to Tk 1.2 lakh and savings certificates up to Tk 5 lakh a year.

> Proofs are not uploaded with the declaration yet: collect them as you do today and verify from them.

## Deposits, certificates and returns

- **Tax deposited**: on a locked run's page, record the challan number, date and amount once the run's tax is paid to the government (within two weeks of the month's end).
- **Salary certificate**: in English or Bangla, from the employee's **Pay** tab or the employee's own **My pay** → **Tax**. It lists each run of the year with salary, tax and challan, and the totals.
- **Download the salary tax register (CSV)** on **Payroll** → **Tax**: the year for everyone.
- **Withholding return (s.177)** on **Payroll** → **Reports**: one CSV per quarter with salary, tax and challans per person. NBR's e-TDS upload template is not public, so it carries what the return asks for, not an exact upload format.

## What to check with your tax consultant

Every tax value in the pack carries its legal reference and is marked for review until a tax consultant signs it off. A month in an income year the pack has no rules for is an error on the run, so tax is never skipped silently. The Tk 1,000 minimum for new taxpayers and the proof of return for supervisory staff (s.264) are not handled yet.
