Every person in a run has a payslip as a PDF in English and in Bangla. Payroll can look at them at any time; employees see theirs only once the run is locked and its payslips are published.

## Check payslips before publishing

On a run's page, open a person and click **Payslip in English** or **Payslip in Bangla**. This works before and after publishing, so you can check a few payslips while the run is still being reviewed.

A payslip shows:

- the company, the employee's code, designation, department and joining date;
- the pay period and the payment date;
- the days: in the period, paid, absent, leave without pay, and overtime hours;
- every earning and deduction, and the net pay in figures and in words (in lakh and crore, or in Bangla words ending in "টাকা মাত্র");
- Bangla digits throughout the Bangla payslip.

## Publish

Once a run is **Locked**, click **Publish payslips** on its page and confirm. A run's payslips are published once; the run list then shows **Payslips published**.

Everyone in the run is told:

- in the inbox, by email and by push to the app, without any amounts;
- by SMS where the workspace has turned SMS on for this event. The SMS goes to the person's own phone and carries their net pay.

HR chooses the channels for **Payslips were published** under **Notifications** → **Channels**, and can reword the message under **Wording**. SMS is off by default and counts against the plan's monthly SMS credits.

> During the parallel months, while your old payroll still pays people, lock the runs but do not publish their payslips or send payment files.

## Where employees read them

Employees with a workspace login linked to their record open **My pay** → **Payslips**. Each payslip shows its period, the payment date and the net pay, with **English PDF** and **Bangla PDF** (their own language first). The same page has their salary tax, provident fund and loans.

In the mobile app, the **Payslips** card lists the same payslips and opens them in the app's language or the other one; the payslip notification opens the list. Factory workers without a smartphone can open theirs on a [kiosk](/help/kiosks).

## Other kinds of payslip

- **Festival bonus** runs: the payslip and the notification carry the festival's name.
- **Adjustment** runs: a separate payslip showing only what changed since the month was locked.
- **Final settlement**: the leaver's statement, with service, notice, unused earned leave, each benefit and the provident fund paid out.
- **Participation fund**: each beneficiary's share, less tax at source.

They are published from their own run's page in the same way.

## If a payslip is wrong

A locked run never changes. Fix the cause (an approved pay change, an attendance correction) and pay the difference with an adjustment: see [Run a payroll month](/help/run-payroll).
