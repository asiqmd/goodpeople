Open **International** to register a BD, AE or SA legal employer. Each company has its own dated country, regime, payroll currency, business time zone and calendar context. The workspace country is an onboarding default.

Registering AE or SA does not authorize statutory payroll. The country packs, cohorts and calendar conversions need qualification. Hijri dates supplied by an employee retain their original value and source; this preview does not convert them into an approved legal date.

For an AE or SA company, **Create an international employee record** accepts an original name, explicit citizenship, international phone number and country-specific contact address. BD parent-name and identity-number fields are not required. Add dated employment context, an issued identifier or bank metadata from the employee's international record. Identity and bank permissions still apply; identifiers are masked and stored encrypted. Saving bank metadata does not initiate a payment.

Changing legal employers requires reviewed service recognition, settlement and opening records. The legacy transfer shortcut is blocked. Historical locked BD results stay unchanged.

Arabic is a technical preview with RTL layout and bundled fonts. Some existing screens and notifications retain explicitly labelled English fallback. Human content and Arabic legal review remain open; Arabic statutory documents are disabled. This guide describes product behaviour, not country-law approval.

USD/AED candidate subscription prices belong to the platform finance area. Checkout stays disabled pending merchant selection, sandbox evidence and finance/tax acceptance. Subscription money is separate from employee payroll and BD invoices; this release does not convert or combine currencies.
