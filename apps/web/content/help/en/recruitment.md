Recruitment comes with the Business plan and up. HR (the workspace admin, HR admins and HR officers) runs it under **Recruitment**. Hiring managers see their own openings, and interviewers see the interviews they are on.

## Openings

An opening is a position you want to fill: the company, unit, location, designation and grade, the employment type, how many people, whether it is new or replaces someone, the monthly gross budget, and the hiring manager.

- HR drafts openings for anyone. A line manager can draft one with themselves as hiring manager.
- **Submit for approval** sends it to the hiring manager's own manager and then HR, through **Approvals**, like leave. Whoever drafted it cannot approve it.
- Once approved, HR writes the **job post**: a title and description in English, Bangla or both, the closing date, and whether to show the salary. **Publish** puts it on your careers page.
- An opening closes on its closing date, when HR closes it, or when all its positions are filled.

## Your careers page

Turn it on under **Recruitment** → **Careers page settings**, with an introduction in both languages, how many months you keep applications (12 unless you change it), and the address candidates write to about their data. Your page is at `/careers/<your workspace>`.

- Each job has its own page, which Google's job search can read: keep the description clear and the closing date right.
- Candidates apply with their name, email, mobile number, a CV (PDF or Word, up to 5 MB) and a note. They must agree to you keeping their application for the period you set, and get a confirmation email.
- bdjobs.com and LinkedIn cannot be posted to automatically. Copy the ready text from the opening's page, and add those candidates by hand with their source.

## Candidates and the pipeline

The opening's **pipeline** shows every application by stage: applied, screening, interview, offer, hired, or rejected and withdrawn with a reason. Every move is recorded with who made it.

- **Add candidate** for walk-ins, referrals, agencies, bdjobs or LinkedIn. Upload the CV and the form fills in the contact details it can read; check them. Tick that the candidate agreed to you keeping their data.
- A person who applies again, or for another job, is found by their email or mobile number, so they stay one candidate.
- CVs typed in older Bangla fonts (Bijoy, SutonnyMJ) cannot be read; type their details.

## Interviews and scorecards

Schedule an interview from the application's page: in person, by phone or by video, with one or more interviewers who have logins. The candidate and the interviewers get a calendar invitation by email, which updates or cancels when the interview does.

Interviewers find their interviews under **Interviews**, with the CV, and fill in a **scorecard**: a rating from 1 to 5 for each of the opening's criteria, a recommendation and notes. An interviewer sees the others' scorecards only after submitting their own, so each score is their own view.

## Offers and hiring

- From the application's page, HR drafts an **offer**: the job, the joining date, the monthly gross, the probation (up to 6 months for clerical work and 3 for other work, Labour Act s.4) and the date it expires, with a letter in English or Bangla.
- An offer above the opening's budget is approved by another HR login first.
- **Send** emails the candidate a private link. They read the letter and accept by typing their name, or decline. The accepted letter is kept with the time and a fingerprint, and the candidate gets a copy.
- **Hire** opens the hire form filled in from the offer. Add what the offer did not have (date of birth and the rest), choose the salary structure, and save. The employee, their job and their documents (the CV and the accepted offer) are created, and the pay is proposed for payroll's approval.
- Issue the appointment letter from **Letters** on the joining day, as the Labour Act requires (s.5).

## Candidates' data

A candidate who was not hired is erased once all their applications have closed and your retention period has passed, unless they joined your talent pool. You can erase a candidate at any time when they ask. Erasing removes their name, contact details, CV, cover note and interviewers' written notes, and keeps the counts and ratings so your hiring figures still add up.
