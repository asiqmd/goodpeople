Everyone signs in on the [sign-in page](/login) with the **Workspace** name their admin gave them (for example `my-group`). The same ways of signing in work in the mobile app.

## With an email and a password

Staff enter the workspace, their email and their password. Passwords have at least 10 characters.

There is no "forgot password" link: a workspace admin makes you a new one-time password on the **Users** page (see below). After too many wrong tries, wait a few minutes and try again.

## With an SMS code (employees)

An employee whose login is a mobile number chooses **Sign in with mobile number**, enters the workspace and the number HR registered, and gets a six-digit code by SMS.

- The code is valid for 5 minutes, allows five wrong guesses and works once.
- The page says the same thing whether or not the number has a login, so nobody can find out who is registered.
- Codes are for employee logins only. Staff roles sign in with a password.

## Two-step sign-in

With two-step sign-in, after your password you also enter a code from an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator. Someone who learns your password still cannot sign in.

To turn it on, open **My account** and choose **Set up two-step sign-in**:

1. Scan the QR code with the app, or type the key shown under it.
2. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows now and choose **Turn on**.
3. Keep the eight recovery codes somewhere safe. Each one signs you in once if you lose your phone, and they are shown only once.

From then on, enter the app's code, or a recovery code, after your password. **My account** shows how many recovery codes you have left. When they run out, turn two-step sign-in off and on again for new ones; if your role must keep it on, ask a workspace admin to reset it.

### When it is required

Where the server requires it, the **Workspace admin**, **HR admin**, **Payroll officer** and **Finance officer** roles must use two-step sign-in. They set it up the next time they sign in, and cannot turn it off. On such a server, a new workspace's owner sets it up straight after sign-up. The **Go-live checklist** and the **Users** page show admins who do not use it yet.

## Your password and language

On **My account** you can change your password: enter the current one and a new one of at least 10 characters. Your other sessions are signed out.

To change your language, use the **English** / **বাংলা** switch at the top of any page. For a workspace login it also sets the language of your email, SMS and app notifications.

## For workspace admins

On the **Users** page, a workspace admin can, for each login:

- **Reset password:** makes a new one-time password, shown only once, to give the person. Their current password stops working and they are signed out. They can change it on **My account**.
- **Reset two-step sign-in:** turns it off, for example after a lost phone. They set it up again the next time it is needed. You cannot reset your own here.
- **Disable:** the login is signed out at once and cannot sign in until it is enabled again.

Nobody changes their own role, and the workspace always keeps at least one active workspace admin.
