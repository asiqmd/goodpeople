Good People is priced per person per month and invoiced after each month with a VAT invoice (Mushak-6.3). Everything here happens on the **Billing** page, which the workspace admin and finance officers can open.

## Plans and prices

Starter, Growth and Business each have a price per staff member, a lower price per factory worker, and a monthly minimum; Enterprise is agreed with us. The [pricing page](/pricing) shows the prices in force, before VAT.

A new workspace starts with a 30-day trial of everything in Business, with no card needed. The banner on every page shows the days left, and the billing people get an email a week before the trial ends, the day before, and when it has ended. When the trial ends, the workspace is read-only until a plan is chosen.

## Choosing a plan

1. Open **Billing**. Under **Choose a plan**, each plan shows its prices and about what this month's people would cost with VAT.
2. Pick a plan and enter the company the invoices go to: its name, address and BIN. The BIN is needed on invoices above Tk 25,000.
3. Enter a **Coupon code** if you have one. A workspace can use one coupon.
4. Select **Choose plan**. The trial becomes the paid plan at once.

You can switch plans later under **Change plan**. The new plan applies from this month's invoice.

## What an invoice counts

Once a month ends, you are invoiced for everyone employed on any day of it. Staff and workers are told apart by their job at the month's end, and **Billing** shows this month's count so far. The first month counts from the day you chose the plan. The minimum, in proportion in a first month, tops up a small month, and a coupon comes off before VAT.

Invoices are numbered like `GP/2026-27/000001` and are usually due ten days after they are issued. Each has a Mushak-6.3 PDF in English and Bangla under **Mushak PDF**. A mistake is corrected with a credit note (Mushak-6.7), never by changing the invoice. If a credit note leaves you in credit on an invoice already paid, our finance team settles it with you.

## Paying

- **Pay by card or internet banking** (SSLCommerz) or **Pay with bKash**: the payment counts once the gateway confirms it to us. These show only where online payment is set up.
- **Pay by bank transfer**: **Billing** shows our bank details. Write the invoice number in the payment reference so we can match it.
- **Cheque**: our finance team records it against the invoice when it reaches us.

Above Tk 1,00,000, pay through a bank or bKash so that your company keeps its VAT input credit.

If your company withholds VAT (VDS) or income tax (TDS) at source, open **Record VAT or tax your company withheld** under the invoice and enter the amount, the certificate number and its date. The deduction counts against the invoice at once, up to the legal rate on the value before VAT. We then check the certificate (Mushak-6.6 for VAT); a deduction we reject is owed again.

## Overdue, read-only and cancelling

The billing people are emailed when an invoice is issued, three days before it is due, when it is overdue (with the date the workspace becomes read-only), when it becomes read-only, and when a payment is received. After the due date a few grace days follow, then the workspace is read-only.

> Read-only is never locked. Everyone can still see and export everything, **Billing** stays open, and paying restores access at once. Nothing is deleted.

To stop, select **Cancel plan**. The plan ends with this month: the month is still invoiced, then the workspace is read-only. You can choose a plan again at any time. A trial has nothing to cancel.

Complimentary, internal, partner and licensed workspaces are not billed by Good People, so **Billing** has nothing to choose or pay. Partner workspaces are invoiced to their partner.
