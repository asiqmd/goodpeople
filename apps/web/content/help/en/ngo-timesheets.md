The NGO pack charges each person's salary to the donor projects they work on. It comes with the Growth plan and up. It never changes what anyone is paid: payroll works as before, and the split happens in the journal and the reports.

## Projects and grants

Under **Projects**, add your donors, then each project. A project belongs to one company and records:

- its donor (none for the organisation's own funds) and the grant reference;
- the NGO Affairs Bureau's FD-6 approval number and date;
- its period;
- the approved budget, and the staff budget within it.

Closing a project ends the salary shares still running on it.

## Salary shares

On a project's page, **Salary shares** plans each person's split: a percent of their salary for the project from a date, as programme or administration cost. A person's shares on any day add up to at most 100%; the rest stays with their own unit. Shares cannot be added or ended over days whose pay is already locked, because the journal has posted them.

## The monthly timesheet

People charged to projects fill in **Timesheets** once a month: days down, projects across, with weekly holidays, holidays, leave and days off marked and the attendance hours beside each day. Hours that are not for a project go under **Other work**.

1. **Fill from attendance** puts each day's worked hours on your projects by your planned shares. Check them.
2. **Submit timesheet** with the declaration that the hours are the work you actually did.
3. Your line manager approves it in **Approvals**, on the web or in the app (HR does, when you have no manager who can sign in). A returned timesheet comes back to you to correct and submit again.

Those who manage projects see everyone's timesheets and can reopen one until that month's pay is locked in payroll. After that, the month's timesheet can no longer be submitted, approved or reopened. In the first five days of each month, people charged to a project who have not submitted last month's timesheet get a reminder.

Field staff can fill in their month in the app without a connection. Open the month once while online; the app sends the entries when a connection returns, and each entry counts once however often it is sent.

## How salaries are charged

For each month, a person's share of a project comes from their approved timesheet: hours on the project over all their hours. Without an approved timesheet, the planned shares are used, weighted by the days each was in force. Every month shows which basis it used. Donors accept planned shares only as an estimate, so approve timesheets before the month's run is locked.

The run's **Journal (CSV)** has a Project column. Expense lines are split per person by the month's shares, to the paisa. Net pay, tax and provident fund are owed to people and authorities, so they are not split.

## Costs and donor reports

A project's page shows **Salaries charged** from locked payroll, month by month: people, full-time equivalents, gross, employer cost, programme and administration, and the basis, with the staff budget against what has been charged so far. Administration over 20% of the approved budget (Foreign Donations Act 2016, s.6) shows a warning; nothing is blocked.

**Donor reports** gives the personnel cost schedule, a row per person and month, as CSV or as a PDF in English or Bangla with a certification for the finance signatory. **Approved timesheets** lists the project's people's timesheets, each as a PDF with the declaration and the approval.

> The 20% cap, and how the approved budget is read for multi-year projects, are still waiting for counsel's review.
