Workspace staff reach Good People support with a ticket, from **Support** in the menu. Employees ask their own HR team instead. Before you write, check the [status page](/status): if a problem is already posted there, we are working on it.

## Open a ticket

Go to [a new ticket](/workspace/support/new) and give:

- a category: **Account and sign-in**, **Billing**, **People and records**, **Attendance**, **Leave**, **Payroll**, **Importing data**, **Something is broken** or **Something else**;
- **Normal** or **Urgent** (see below);
- a subject and a description of what happened, what you expected and what you tried;
- the **Error reference**, if a page showed one, and the **Page** where it happened;
- files if they help: up to 5 per message, 10 MB each, as PNG, JPEG or WebP pictures, PDF, Excel (`.xlsx`), CSV or text.

The ticket gets a number such as `GP-1001`. You see the tickets you opened; the workspace admin sees every ticket the workspace has raised. Contacting support works even while the workspace is read-only.

> When something goes wrong, the error message gives a reference, a string of letters and digits such as `7f3c9a1e…`. Copy it into the ticket: it points us to exactly that request.

## Normal or urgent

Choose urgent only when payroll cannot run, nobody can sign in, or attendance has stopped arriving. Everything else is normal.

Our first answer is due within these support hours, by plan (normal, then urgent):

- Starter: 16 and 8 hours
- Growth: 8 and 4 hours
- Business, and trials: 4 and 2 hours
- Enterprise: 2 and 1 hour

Support hours are Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 18:00 Dhaka time. Time outside them does not count, so a ticket opened on Thursday evening is due on Sunday.

If nobody in the workspace can sign in at all, write to the address at the foot of this page.

## Replies and statuses

When support replies, the person who opened the ticket gets an email in their language with a link. Read and answer on the ticket's page. A ticket is:

- **Open** while it waits on us, including after you reply;
- **Waiting for you** when we have asked something;
- **Solved** when we think it is fixed. Reply and it opens again; otherwise it closes by itself after 7 days. When it is fixed for you, choose **This is solved** yourself;
- **Closed** when it is finished. A new problem needs a new ticket.

## Letting support look inside

Some problems are quicker to solve when support sees what you see. The workspace admin can let support in from **Support** → [Support access](/workspace/support/access), with **Let support in**:

- read-only, for 1 to 7 days, for a ticket or in general, with a note;
- support can look but not change anything, and identity numbers stay hidden;
- the admin who allowed it gets an email each time someone from support opens the workspace;
- every page support opens is recorded in the workspace's audit log, and listed under **What support opened** on the same page;
- the admin can **Revoke** the access at any time, and support is signed out at once.

Without that consent, support never sees inside your workspace.
