This policy explains how you stop paying for Good People and when you get money back. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It is also published in Bangla; if the English and Bangla texts differ, the English text prevails.

## Trials

A trial lasts 30 days, includes everything in the Business plan and needs no card, so there is nothing to pay or refund. When it ends, the workspace becomes read-only until you choose a plan. Nothing is deleted.

## How you are billed

- You pay after each month, never in advance. Once a month ends, you are invoiced for everyone employed on any day of it: staff at the staff price and factory workers at the worker price, with the plan's monthly minimum.
- A first month counts from the day you chose the plan.
- Each invoice is a Mushak-6.3 tax invoice in English and Bangla, and is due ten days after it is issued unless it shows another date.

## Cancelling

- A workspace admin or finance officer can cancel the plan at any time on the workspace's Billing page.
- The plan ends with the month you cancel in. That month is invoiced as usual, and no invoice follows it.
- The workspace then becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you can still see your data and download what the workspace offers. You can choose a plan again on the Billing page at any time.

Because you pay only for months that have ended, cancelling never leaves an unused period to refund.

## When we refund or credit you

- **A billing mistake.** If an invoice counts people who were not employed in the month, uses the wrong price, or is wrong in any other way, tell us. We issue a credit note (Mushak-6.7) against the invoice, with its VAT.
- **Paying twice or too much.** If the same invoice is paid twice, or you pay more than it asks, we refund the difference or set it against your next invoice, as you prefer.
- **A serious failure of the service.** If Good People was unavailable for a significant part of a month because of a fault on our side, tell us within 30 days of that month's invoice. We credit or refund a fair part of that month's charge.

A credit note on an invoice you have already paid leaves you in credit. Our finance team then sets the credit against your next invoice or refunds it, as you prefer.

## When we do not refund

- Months that have ended and in which the workspace was available to use.
- Periods in which the workspace was read-only because a trial ended or an invoice was not paid.
- VAT or income tax your company withheld at source (VDS or TDS). Record the deduction against the invoice with its certificate instead: it reduces what you owe at once.

## How refunds are paid

- We pay a refund back the way you paid: to the card or bank account used through SSLCommerz, to the bKash account used, or by bank transfer for payments made by transfer or cheque.
- We start the refund within 7 working days of agreeing it. Your bank or wallet may take a few more days to show it.
- Refunds are in taka, for the amount paid, and come with a credit note so your VAT records stay right.

## How to ask

Raise a billing ticket from your workspace, or write to the address at the foot of this page. Give the invoice number (such as `GP/2026-27/000001`) and what you think is wrong.
