## 2. The service

Good People is software for human resources, attendance, leave and payroll, used through a workspace for your organisation on the web, in the mobile app, on kiosks and through the Edge Agent at your sites. What your workspace includes depends on its plan, as shown on the [pricing page](/pricing).

Good People applies Bangladesh's labour, tax and VAT rules as we have encoded them, each with its legal reference. Some rules are marked as awaiting review by our lawyers or tax consultant. The software does not give legal or tax advice. You remain the employer: you are responsible for complying with the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, the Income Tax Act 2023 and every other law that applies to you, and for checking pay, tax and deductions before you pay people or file returns.

We improve the service over time. We tell workspaces in advance before we take away something they use.

## 3. Trials

- A new workspace starts with a free 30-day trial of the Business plan. No card is needed.
- Each email address can start one trial.
- When the trial ends, the workspace becomes read-only until a plan is chosen on its Billing page. Nothing is deleted.

## 4. Accounts and security

- You decide who has a login in your workspace and what each role can do. A workspace admin manages logins on the Users page.
- Keep passwords and sign-in codes secret. Use two-step sign-in for admins and anyone who handles pay; our server can require it for them.
- You are responsible for what is done with your workspace's logins. Tell us at once if you think a login has been misused.
- Our support staff can see your workspace only when a workspace admin grants access: read-only, for at most 7 days at a time, ended early whenever the admin chooses, and with every page they open recorded in your workspace's audit log.

## 5. Acceptable use

You will use Good People only for your organisation's lawful employment, HR and payroll purposes, and only with personal data you are allowed to process. You will not:

- try to reach another workspace's data or get round the limits of a role or plan;
- test, probe or attack the service's security without our written permission;
- upload malicious files or content you have no right to use;
- put a load on the service that harms other customers, or copy the software;
- resell or share the service outside your organisation, unless you are our partner under a written agreement.

We may limit or suspend access that puts the service, other customers or the people in your workspace at risk. Where we can, we tell you first and explain why.

## 8. Prices, invoices and payment

- Prices are per staff member and per factory worker a month, with a monthly minimum, as shown on the pricing page when the month starts. Enterprise prices are agreed with us.
- You are invoiced after each month for everyone employed on any day of that month. A first month counts from the day the plan was chosen. A coupon comes off before VAT.
- VAT is added as the law requires, and each invoice is a Mushak-6.3 tax invoice. If your company withholds VAT (VDS) or income tax (TDS), record it against the invoice with its certificate; it counts against what you owe at once.
- An invoice is due ten days after it is issued unless it shows another date. You can pay by card or internet banking (through SSLCommerz), by bKash, by bank transfer or by cheque. Above Tk 1,00,000, pay through a bank or mobile financial service so your company keeps its VAT input credit.
- If an invoice is not paid by its due date, we send reminders. After a grace period, the workspace becomes read-only until it is paid. Paying restores it at once.
- We announce price changes in advance. A new price applies from the first month that starts after it takes effect.
- Refunds follow our [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/legal/refunds).
