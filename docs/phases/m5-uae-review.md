## Evidence to obtain

- Original consolidated Arabic/English law and amendments, executive regulations and
  current notices, with actual file hashes and instrument/effective-date reconciliation.
- Named local payroll/legal review per rule, cohort and version, with worked boundaries:
  join/leave, unpaid/sick leave, ordinary/night/shift/rest/holiday/Ramadan, exclusions,
  deduction aggregation/consent, pension history/basis/caps/support, service fractions,
  leap/year boundaries, resignation/termination, notice, carry/encashment and deadline.
- Three consecutive real pilot periods and a final settlement compared employee by employee
  with independently prepared payroll. Synthetic arithmetic fixtures remain technical tests.
- Current bank/exchange-agent schema and valid synthetic samples; provider-accepted batch,
  rejection/correction, upload reference and reconciled return evidence. A generic or old
  public-guide file does not complete this gate. Real export needs approved UAE payroll.
- English and Arabic payslip/letter/consent/retention review, and privacy, hosting/transfer
  and candidate-data retention/purge decisions. Preview PDFs are not reviewed statutory
  documents. Do not advertise full Arabic translation or compliance certification.
- Country qualification for actual leave/attendance/payroll/settlement ledgers, scheduled
  work, reports/journals, payments, recruitment/compensation/project workflows and employee
  self-service. None are enabled by this manual preview namespace.
