2. Configure each required control's named internal coordinator, procedure, maximum permitted cadence, classification, approved retention and execution due date. 3. Reference a real redacted artifact, its SHA-256, observed execution time, covered period, result and limitations. Preserve original evidence privately under agreed retention and legal holds. 5. Assign actual findings and due dates. Remediation requires accepted matching evidence. The accountable owner must make any independently attributable, compensated, expiring risk decision. Unowned/overdue and open critical/high findings block; legal/provider/host requirements remain independent. Only explicitly waivable governance controls permit a reviewed exception, at most 30 days; risk decisions expire within 90 days.
6. Record actual CPA report metadata only with accepted external proof, matching document hash/period and restricted distribution. It does not issue an examination opinion or enable public advertising. No report is recorded in this local candidate.
7. A second administrator can recommend scoped engineering review only when every current gate passes, reviewing the exact latest chain head. Any later append revokes that recommendation's currency. Export the restricted unsigned record and keep the actual evidence and independent decision with it.
