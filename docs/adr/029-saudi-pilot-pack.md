# ADR 029: Saudi preview pack and qualification evidence

Date: 3 November 2026. Status: accepted for a local candidate; statutory/provider release remains unqualified.

M6 uses a separate `sa` calculator, manifest, encrypted evidence namespace and `/tenant/saudi` / `/workspace/saudi` surface. It reuses M5's scoped evidence pattern without importing UAE classifications, pension support, basic-only gratuity or EDR/SCR file records. The existing BD and AE consumers remain unchanged and SA's normal statutory registry remains unreviewed.

Coverage is explicit, dated and versioned. The candidate supports standard full-time HRSD private-sector employment with work inside Saudi Arabia, a single insured employer, an evidenced fixed cash wage and a written Gregorian agreement. Non-Saudi contracts require an explicit fixed term/end date; supported Saudi indefinite monthly contracts require recorded notice coverage. Probation is recorded in days. Unknown, special/government/domestic regimes, GCC extension, cross-border, variable pay, multiple insured employers and Hijri-dependent cases require separate review. Supplied Hijri dates are not silently converted.

GOSI requires insured-status attestation, authority, branches, earliest Social Insurance/Civil Pension participation, contribution salary/date and the documented occupational-hazards rate. Legacy, new-law and non-GCC hazards-only cases remain distinct. Current joining date does not select a law. The bounded June–November 2026 preview models the new-cohort July rate boundary and itemizes annuity, SANED and hazards amounts with no UAE government support. Partial/reduced insured months block until independently reviewed rather than guessing contribution proration.

Financial amounts use integer halalas, with half-up component rounding. Calendar-day salary proration, a 240-hour overtime divisor, conservative standard-time/annual overtime guards and Gregorian anniversary service fractions are proposals requiring named local review. Overtime uses supplied hourly fixed wage plus half basic hourly wage. There is no automatic compensatory leave, work authorization or legality decision. Ordinary deduction category and aggregate guards include employee contributions.

Sick leave previews full/75%/unpaid bands. Annual leave returns an annual rate, not an accrued balance. Maternity is a standard-duration preview without GOSI benefit/salary coordination; event, Hajj and exam eligibility are explicitly attested. No leave record or medical decision is made. Settlements use final fixed wage, proportional recognized single-employer service and Saudi resignation factors. An Article 87 exception requires a distinct evidence reference. Disputed Article 80/77 claims, offsets, variable pay and insurance settlement are outside this candidate; a recorded contract-expiry reason must match the actual contract end date.

Migrations 0068–0069 add seven tenant/company-bound tables with FORCE RLS, composite references, dated overlap guards and append-only financial evidence. Field encryption binds tenant, field namespace and row identity. Parent locks and monotonic sequences order comparisons and simulated returns. MFA, payroll entitlement/rights, company/relationship scope and all HR field read grants protect the surface; M1 custom payroll assignment restrictions are retained.

Comparisons reconcile all employees and all six monetary fields against source values and a source-document hash. Independent comparisons require a second login; synthetic comparisons cannot qualify real pilot periods. Historical snapshots remain readable but stale contexts cannot be reused for comparisons/new sandbox files. Attestations do not activate the pack.

The wage-file artifact is deliberately an internal reconciliation contract, not an accepted bank payroll instruction or sealed Mudad file. It contains no real bank, government or employer identifiers and is marked non-uploadable. Exact-byte hashes, line/control totals, ordered simulated upload/accept/reject/reconcile events and replay conflicts are recorded. Payment is never inferred. A pilot-specific bank adapter, digitally signed/sealed file, actual bank results, Mudad processing/justifications and payment reconciliation remain external release gates. No Qiwa, Mudad, GOSI or e-invoicing portal integration is implied.

Every rule remains `needs_review`, with definition hashes separate from original source-document hashes. No named reviewer, original consolidated hash or real pilot/bank acceptance is invented. See [the source/review dossier](../phases/m6-saudi-review.md). Next implementation: M7 assurance and enterprise release, carrying all earlier operating gates forward.
