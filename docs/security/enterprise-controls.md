## M5 UAE candidate evidence

The UAE preview namespace retains M4 statutory boundaries, all-field read/MFA controls,
company/relationship checks and M1 delegation restrictions. Seven force-RLS tables use
composite tenant/company references; row-bound encrypted worker/financial/evidence payloads,
append-only records, dated coverage, current-context checks, parent locks and sequences
protect the technical evidence flow. Sandbox file hashes and replay conflicts are retained;
no accepted/reconciled event asserts payment. [ADR 028](../adr/028-uae-pilot-pack.md) and the
[UAE review dossier](../phases/m5-uae-review.md) identify unqualified consumers, retention,
local-law, source, pilot, document, hosting/transfer and bank-agent gates. These controls are
implementation evidence and do not certify UAE compliance or activate the country engine.

## M7 restricted assurance register and actual gaps

[ADR 030](../adr/030-assurance-and-enterprise-release.md) and [the M7 operating/review dossier](../phases/m7-assurance-review.md) implement the register without asserting control operation. Two encrypted, append-only platform FORCE-RLS tables, current Super Admin/recent MFA, independent version-bound reviews, full-period/freshness checks, owned expiring decisions and exact-head release recommendations protect the local workflow. Exports and installed bundles verify canonical source/event hashes; they remain dependent on accountable issuer custody and truthful original evidence.

The actual native program has 18 required controls and zero assigned human owners. The overdue backup/offsite/recovery ownership finding is high and open. uuid GHSA-w5hq-g745-h8pq and minimatch GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249 are moderate and open; the high/critical audit threshold passes without closing them. Named triage and remediation/justified time-bounded risk decisions are pending. The old backup records/alert remain intact. Three system worker tasks are healthy at observation; physical recovery, offsite custody and HA/failover remain unqualified.

Synthetic review is demonstrably separate from operating evidence. No independent pentest, real incident/tabletop, legal/privacy approval, CPA report or signed commercial release was performed. Existing M1-M6 gates carry forward. AE/SA normal consumers and merchant checkout remain disabled. Named management/security/operations/privacy/release ownership and agreed criteria, period, retention and incident/RTO/RPO targets are prerequisites for closure, not presumed approvals.
