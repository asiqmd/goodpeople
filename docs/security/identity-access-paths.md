Custom payroll permission assignments require all-company scope until historical financial resources carry reliable company snapshots. Other own/direct-report permission types are refused. Final grant removal produces none, never the legacy empty-array meaning of all. Own service remains independent; disabling the login stops both.

The two-instance suite certifies these identity paths, not the timers/notification delivery scheduler. Continue the single-runner deployment limit into M2. External provider behavior, customer account migration, independent security review and actual production evidence remain release gates.
