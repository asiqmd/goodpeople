# Security review, October 2026

Our own review before the third-party penetration test (Phase 3 M5), against OWASP ASVS 4.0
level 2. It covered the code as it stood before M5's new support, status and help features;
those were reviewed as they were built, and the testers cover them
([pentest-scope.md](pentest-scope.md)).

**How it was done.**

- **Tracing:** each finding was traced from route to guard, service and query, then checked
  again before it was fixed. Two were confirmed with scripts: the form-parsing prototype
  pollution and the spreadsheet decompression.
- **Dependencies:** `pnpm audit --prod` was run.

**Result:**

- 2 high, 9 medium and 9 low findings, and 7 informational notes.
- **Fixed:** both highs, eight mediums and five lows, and one more low in part (L8).
- **Accepted for launch:** one medium (M7) and two lows (L4, L7), with their reasons below.
- **Open:** one low (L9).
  The launch gate is no open high or critical, and every medium fixed or accepted in writing
  ([launch.md](../ops/launch.md#security-eng-security-contact)).

## How each was handled

- **H1:** hiring may give only the `employee` role unless the login may manage logins
  (`users.manage`); otherwise 403 `role_needs_users_manage`. Linking a login to an existing
  employee already needed `users.manage`.
- **H2:** `nest()` skips `__proto__`, `prototype` and `constructor` in form field names.
- **M1:** each zip entry of a spreadsheet is inflated with a hard ceiling before it is
  parsed: 500 entries, 32 MB unpacked, and at most 200 to 1 for entries over 1 MB. Otherwise
  400 `spreadsheet_too_large`.
- **M2:**
  - The wrong-PIN lock is per kiosk and worker, so the worker can use another kiosk.
  - Each kiosk allows 30 wrong PINs in 15 minutes across all workers, then pauses its
    sign-ins for 5 minutes.
- **M3:** requests to the speak-up pages are logged with no address, user or workspace, and
  Caddy does not log them at all.
- **M4:**
  - Forming or changing the complaint committee (s.332A) needs the workspace admin
    (`users.manage`).
  - With no committee, harassment cases are visible only to workspace admins, never to every
    grievance handler.
- **M5:** the sign-up email no longer contains anything the visitor typed.
- **M6:** the Edge Agent accepts devices only from `GP_DEVICE_ALLOW` (addresses or ranges) and
  `GP_DEVICE_SERIALS` when set, and warns at start when neither is. The site go-live sets them.
- **M7, accepted:**
  - Chromium cannot run its sandbox under Docker's default profile.
  - The risk is narrowed already:
    - pages have JavaScript off, and every request other than `data:` is blocked;
    - the API runs as a non-root user with every capability dropped;
    - templates escape their content.
  - Before an enterprise or bank customer: render PDFs in their own container without the
    keys, or allow Chromium's namespaces with a seccomp profile
    ([runbook.md → Chrome for PDFs](../ops/runbook.md#chrome-for-pdfs)).
- **M8:** with `NODE_ENV=production` the API refuses to start when:
  - `JWT_ACCESS_SECRET` or `FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is the value shipped in `.env.example`;
  - `BILLING_FAKE_GATEWAY` is on;
  - email or SMS would only be written to the log, unless `ALLOW_LOG_SENDERS=on` (staging).
- **M9:**
  - A change of the primary payout account notifies the employee by email, SMS and push. The
    message shows the last four digits only, and the workspace cannot switch it off.
  - The change is audited as `employee.primary_account.change`.
  - The payroll run's review marks everyone whose account changed since the last locked run,
    for the approver to check.
- **L1:** text cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return get a
  leading `'`. Signed numbers stay as they are.
- **L2:** each two-step code works once per login, and second-step tokens are single-use.
  This memory is per process; a restart forgets it.
- **L3:** a refresh revokes the old session with a conditional update. Of two at the same
  moment, one fails; a later reuse is still treated as a leak and signs everyone out.
- **L4, accepted:** the per-login limit stops guessing, and a locked person waits 15 minutes.
  The per-address limit stays. Revisit with backoff, or a step-up, when the rate limits move
  to a shared store.
- **L5:** maternity lists, cases and candidates, and unions with their check-off files, follow
  the login's company limit. Committee lists do not yet (informational: committees are
  per-company records that any compliance login already manages).
- **L6:** ten coupon tries per workspace an hour, and the coupon row is locked while a
  redemption is counted.
- **L7, accepted:** access tokens last 15 minutes, and disabling a login ends its refresh
  sessions at once. For a sacked admin, also rotate `JWT_ACCESS_SECRET`
  ([launch.md → Security incidents](../ops/launch.md#security-incidents)).
- **L8:**
  - Fixed: the backup scripts pass database passwords through the environment of each
    command, never its arguments, and S3 keys likewise.
  - Open: off-site copies are not encrypted by the scripts, so they must go to an encrypted
    destination ([runbook.md → Known gaps](../ops/runbook.md#known-gaps)).
- **L9, open:** the five-guess limit on a sign-up code can be overrun by a few parallel
  guesses, and two parallel verifications of one sign-up could both create a workspace.
  Fix with one locked transaction before sign-up traffic grows.

## Informational

- **I1, dependencies:** fixed. `multer` (high) is overridden to 2.5.0. `uuid` (moderate, via
  ExcelJS) and `esbuild` (moderate, development only) remain. CI's `security` job fails on
  any high or critical.
- **I2, the web's CSP:** it allows inline scripts, which Next.js needs for hydration. Nonces
  would remove that; not done.
- **I3, `/docs`:** off in production unless `API_DOCS=on`.
- **I4, compose:** PostgreSQL and MinIO run without `cap_drop`. They listen on 127.0.0.1
  only.
- **I5, platform staff:** they have no disable switch; remove the account. A follow-up.
- **I6, enumeration:** workspace addresses can be guessed from sign-up and sign-in. Low value.
- **I7, done well:**
  - AES-256-GCM per field;
  - argon2id passwords;
  - hashed refresh tokens;
  - constant-time comparison of kiosk and agent tokens;
  - forced row-level security with an app role that cannot bypass it;
  - storage keys that cannot leave their folder;
  - payments verified server to server and settled once.

## Areas found sound

- **Isolation and access control:**
  - Tenant isolation on every request path.
  - Permission checks on every workspace route.
  - The self-service and per-person routes against other people's records.
- **Payments:** the SSLCommerz and bKash callbacks.
- **Sessions:** JWT handling (algorithm, secret, token types), and the web's cookies.
- **Injection:** no raw SQL on request paths, and escaping in PDFs and letters.
- **Anonymity:** grievance storage keeps no reporter details.
